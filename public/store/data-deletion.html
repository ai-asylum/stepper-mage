Data Deletion

Last updated 13 August 2026

How to have data associated with your device deleted from Unbound Descent (Android package games.misaligned.unbounddescent), operated by Misaligned.

There is no account to delete

Unbound Descent has no sign-in and no user accounts. We hold no name, email address or contact detail for you, so there is no profile to close — only the anonymous analytics and attribution records described in our Privacy Policy.

Delete your saved progress yourself

Your progress is stored only on your device and never reaches us. To erase it:

  • Android app — Settings → Apps → Unbound Descent → Storage → Clear storage, or simply uninstall the app.
  • Browser — clear site data for this domain in your browser settings.

This is immediate and irreversible, and it does not require us.

Request deletion of analytics and attribution data

Email support@misaligned.games with the subject Data deletion request — Unbound Descent.

So that we can find your records, include your device’s advertising ID (Android Settings → Privacy → Ads — the ID is shown at the bottom of that screen). Attribution data is keyed to it, and we will delete it via our provider.

What gets deleted, and what cannot be found

  • Attribution data (AppsFlyer) — keyed to your advertising ID, so it can be located and deleted on request.
  • Gameplay analytics (PostHog) — stored under a random in-app identifier that is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID. We cannot look it up from the outside, which is also what stops it identifying you; it is deleted automatically on our retention schedule.
  • On-device progress — never sent to us, so it is yours to delete using the steps above.

How long it takes

We action deletion requests within 30 days and reply to confirm when it is done.

Doing it without us

You can sever attribution at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. New events will no longer tie to the old ID.
