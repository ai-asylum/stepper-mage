Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

This is the privacy policy for Unbound Descent (Android package games.misaligned.unbounddescent), operated by Misaligned ("we", "us"). Questions: support@misaligned.games.

There are no accounts. You do not sign in, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other contact detail in order to play.

What the Android app collects

  • PostHog analytics (EU-hosted) — anonymous product-analytics events, an anonymous device identifier, and approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address. Not tied to your name or email; we do not sell it.
  • AppsFlyer install attribution — your device’s advertising ID (GAID) and basic install/app-open events, to measure which ad campaigns lead to installs (the app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission). AppsFlyer processes this on our behalf; aggregate campaign results are shared with our advertising partners (e.g. AppLovin). We do not use it to build an advertising profile of you. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What the browser version collects

Nothing. The web build at this domain ships without an analytics key, so no product analytics and no attribution run there at all.

What stays on your device

Your progress — unlocks, stars and settings — is stored in your device’s local storage and never leaves it. We cannot read it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, deletes it permanently.

Why we collect it

To see which parts of the game people play and where they stop, and to measure which ad campaigns lead to installs. We do not build advertising profiles from this data.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Analytics data is processed by PostHog on EU infrastructure and attribution data by AppsFlyer, both on our behalf. Aggregate campaign results — counts, not individuals — are shared with advertising partners.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit. Analytics events are deleted automatically on our retention schedule, and we delete attribution data associated with your device on request.

Children

Unbound Descent is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

You can sever attribution yourself at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. To have data deleted, see Data Deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
