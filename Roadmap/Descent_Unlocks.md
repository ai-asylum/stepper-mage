# Descent Unlocks

**Player-facing:** yes
**Started:** —
**Status:** planned

Boss kills unlock deeper start points, offered every fifth floor.

## Why this phase

Re-treading early floors becomes a chore as the dungeon grows. Deed-gating on boss kills means the player can only start somewhere they have proven they can reach.
